## What Kuyo provides

Kuyo reads messages from the Discord channels a server administrator selects aloud in a connected voice channel. It also gives a short in-character reply, sometimes with an emoji reaction, when a user explicitly mentions it. These replies are generated by a language model, are kept deliberately brief, and are not advice, factual answers, or statements by the operator. Kuyo is a fictional adult character, and character replies may respond to sexually explicit messages. Short-term context may be used to continue a conversation, and members who turn on Kuyo memory let Kuyo keep short facts about them in each server for later conversations. Kuyo also provides mini-games — rock-paper-scissors, a Korean word chain, a ladder draw, and a winner draw — that any member can open with `/game`. Games run in the channel where they are opened, hold nothing beyond the running game, and their outcomes are drawn at random for entertainment only; they are not a wager, a prize promise, or any other binding decision. Server administrators choose where TTS works and can disable individual commands in Discord's integration settings, and users can control their own voice preferences. When the operator runs the optional settings website, the same settings can be changed there after signing in with Discord, with the same permissions as in Discord.
